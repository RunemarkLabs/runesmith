{COMPANY} - AIOPS Best Practices

Plan before you build

Every substantive change starts as a plan with a decisions log. Decisions are recorded the day they're made, with who made them.

Decisions are structured prompts

When Claude needs a decision, it asks with choices, the recommended one first. Never a freeform "should I proceed?". It asks only when the answer changes what gets built.

Approval before any write

No Jira ticket, Confluence page, push or promotion happens without the owner's approval. Drafts stay local until then.

One goal at a time

A goal line names the task comms and their outcome, and always ends: "Done when each task's Acceptance is met, or each item that needs the owner is named in the reply as an owner action." Changes to a running goal go in a new task.

Verify, don't trust

The planner checks every reply against the repository: the branch, the commit, the build tag, the changelog, the screenshots. A reply that doesn't match gets a fix task, never an edit.

Promote only on the owner's word

One step of the chain per promotion. A hold on beta stays until the owner lifts it. Overrides are recorded in the owner's own words.

Secrets stay out of chat and out of repos

Credentials are pasted into files, never into chat. Skills report a key as set or missing, never its value. No credentials file inside a repo folder.

Scope every lookup

Confluence title searches cross spaces; always pass space-id={SPACE_ID}. Jira searches use POST /rest/api/3/search/jql.

Use canonical tokens

{COMPANY}, {SITE}, {SPACE_KEY}, {SPACE_ID}, {PROJECT_KEY}. No synonyms such as ORG, TEAM or TENANT.

No AI attribution

Commits, pull requests and files carry no AI co-author lines or generated-by footers. The head's settings and commit hook enforce it.
