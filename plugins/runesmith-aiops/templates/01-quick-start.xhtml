The model in one paragraph

The owner decides. The planner (Claude in a Claude Project) plans the work, writes tasks for the builder, checks the builder's work against the repository, and talks to Jira and Confluence. The builder (Claude Code) writes the code, one goal at a time, and reports back through files in the project's comms/ folder. Builds move from dev to qa to beta to main only on the owner's word.
